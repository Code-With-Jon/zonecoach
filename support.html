Get help

Questions, problems or ideas: send us a message (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include your iPhone and Apple Watch models and the iOS and watchOS versions.

Common questions

The heart rate shows "--" during a workout

ZoneCoach needs permission to record heart rate. On iPhone, open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › ZoneCoach and turn on all categories. Wear the watch snugly with Wrist Detection on.

My steps or weight don't match Apple Health

ZoneCoach reads Apple Health when the app opens and every minute while it's open. Make sure ZoneCoach is allowed to read Steps and Body Weight in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › ZoneCoach.

Blood pressure from my cuff doesn't appear

Turn on Blood Pressure for ZoneCoach in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › ZoneCoach. Some iOS versions leave it off the first permission screen.

Why is my zone marked "Estimated"?

Some inputs are missing, such as age, sex or resting heart rate. Add them in the Health app or on ZoneCoach's Profile tab.

How do I start a workout?

Tap a goal in ZoneCoach on Apple Watch, use Start on Apple Watch on the iPhone's Live tab, tap the complication, or say "Start ZoneCoach" to Siri.

ZoneCoach Pro

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open Profile › Restore purchases.

Your data

ZoneCoach keeps everything on your devices. Export or delete it in Profile › Data & privacy. Read the privacy policy.

Not medical advice

ZoneCoach gives general fitness guidance. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise program.
