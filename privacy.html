ZoneCoach Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

The short version

ZoneCoach doesn't collect your data. Everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What ZoneCoach uses, and where it stays

  • Apple Health. With your permission, ZoneCoach reads your age, sex, height, weight, resting heart rate, heart rate, blood pressure, steps, exercise minutes, workouts, VO₂ max, and the calories and macronutrients you log. It writes the height, weight and blood pressure you enter, and the workouts you record with ZoneCoach. You can change these permissions at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › ZoneCoach.
  • Your profile and history. Your profile, goals, weigh-ins, blood pressure readings, intake estimate and workout history are stored in ZoneCoach's private storage on your iPhone. The settings your watch needs are sent directly to your paired Apple Watch.
  • Motion. Motion data on Apple Watch is used to count steps live.

What we never do

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, sold, shared with third parties, or stored in iCloud. ZoneCoach has no servers.

Purchases

ZoneCoach Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and ZoneCoach only checks whether your subscription is active. We don't receive your payment details. Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Your control

Export everything ZoneCoach has stored (Profile › Data & privacy › Export my data), or delete it all (Profile › Data & privacy › Delete all my data). Deleting the app also deletes its data. Data ZoneCoach saved in Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app, or with the option in Delete all my data.

Children

ZoneCoach is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

ZoneCoach gives general fitness guidance based on standard formulas. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have high blood pressure or a heart condition.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new effective date. For questions, use the Support link on ZoneCoach's App Store page.
