ZoneCoach Health & Safety Terms

Version 2026-10-25

  • ZoneCoach gives general fitness and wellness guidance. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
  • Heart-rate zones, calories, weight projections and plans are estimates from standard formulas and your sensors, and can be wrong.
  • Check with your doctor first if you have a heart or blood pressure condition, take medication that affects heart rate, are pregnant, have a history of eating disorders, or haven't exercised in a while.
  • Stop right away if you feel chest pain, pressure, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath or a racing or irregular heartbeat. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
  • You exercise at your own risk.

General wellness only

ZoneCoach provides general fitness and wellness information to help you exercise at a comfortable intensity and build healthy habits. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease or condition. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always seek the advice of your doctor with questions about a medical condition, and never ignore or delay medical advice because of something in ZoneCoach.

Talk to your doctor first if

  • you have, or think you may have, a heart, lung, blood pressure or metabolic condition
  • you have a pacemaker or other implanted device
  • you take medication that affects your heart rate or blood pressure (for example beta blockers)
  • you are pregnant or recently gave birth
  • you have or had an eating disorder (don't use the calorie or weight features without your care team)
  • you are under 18 (use ZoneCoach only with a parent's or guardian's permission)
  • you haven't exercised regularly, or are returning after illness or injury

Stop exercising if you feel

chest pain, pressure or tightness; dizziness or light-headedness; fainting; unusual shortness of breath; a racing, pounding or irregular heartbeat; nausea; or pain in your arm, jaw, neck or back. If symptoms don't go away, or are severe, call your local emergency number. ZoneCoach can't detect or respond to emergencies.

Estimates, not measurements

Maximum heart rate, training zones, blood pressure categories, calorie burn, intake targets, weight projections, step counts and plans are estimates from standard formulas and from data provided by Apple Watch, Apple Health and you. Sensor readings can be inaccurate or missing. Haptic cues may be late, missed or wrong. Don't rely on ZoneCoach to keep you safe; listen to your body.

Weight and calories

Weight projections and intake targets are general estimates for healthy adults and are limited to about 1% of body weight a week. They aren't a diet plan or nutrition advice for you. Don't eat below a safe intake, and get professional advice for any medical diet.

Your responsibility

You choose whether, how and how hard to exercise, and you do so at your own risk. You're responsible for checking with your doctor and for your own safety, including your surroundings, equipment and the weather.
